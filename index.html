Fragile Freight: Delivery Under Pressure

A 2D physics delivery game for Android by Abaza APPs.

Drive a scooter, a pickup or a heavy hauler over mountain roads, and get fragile cargo to the goal in one piece. Earn gems and stars across 20 contracts, then upgrade your vehicles for harder routes.
