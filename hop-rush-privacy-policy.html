Hop & Rush – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Android game Hop & Rush (package com.m7mdabaza.hopandrush, published on Google Play by Abaza APPs, "we") handles information. In short: the game does not ask for your name, email or any account, and we have no servers of our own. Your progress stays on your phone. Ads, game statistics and crash reports are provided by Google, which handles some data as described below.

1. Information stored only on your device

To work, the game saves the following on your phone. It is never sent to us:

  • Your progress: best height, coins, gems, rockets, upgrades, skins and trails, daily missions, the daily reward, achievements and lifetime statistics (runs, heights, coins collected).
  • Settings: music, sound, volume levels, vibration, screen shake and tap to jump.
  • Technical counters, such as when an ad or the free gift was last shown.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this game data in your device backup, so it can be restored on a new phone. This backup is managed by Google and your device settings, not by us.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob): optional videos that you choose to watch for a reward (for example a revive or double coins), and occasional full-screen ads between runs. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • Interactions with ads and the game (for example ad views and taps, app launches);
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data.

The SDK may also use Android's privacy-preserving advertising features (Privacy Sandbox). This information is collected and used by Google, not by us, and is sent over encrypted connections. You can learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Game statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and to fix problems, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics:

  • Game statistics (Firebase Analytics): events about play, such as a run starting or ending with its height and coins, missions completed, items bought with in-game coins or gems, settings changed and ads shown. These events contain no name, email or other personal details. Firebase also records general information such as the device model, Android version, app version, language, country (estimated from the IP address) and a random app instance ID.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): if the game crashes, a report with technical details (the error, the device model, Android version, memory state and a random installation ID) is sent so we can fix the problem.

This information is processed by Google on our behalf. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

4. Your consent and choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other regions where it is required, the game asks for your consent using Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy options (shown where this applies).
  • On your phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in Android Settings (Privacy / Ads, or Google → Ads, depending on the device).
  • You can manage how Google personalizes ads in My Ad Center.
  • Watching a video ad is always optional; the whole game can be played without them.

5. Purchases

The game currently has no purchases with real money. Coins and gems are earned by playing. If paid items are added later, they will be sold through Google Play, and this policy will be updated first.

6. What the game does not do

The game does not require an account or sign-in, and does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location. It uses the vibration of your phone only for game feedback (you can turn it off in Settings). We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the Google services described above, no data is shared with third parties.

7. Children

Hop & Rush is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help.

8. Keeping and deleting data

  • Data on your device stays until you delete it. You can remove all game data by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the game.
  • Data collected by Google for ads, statistics and crash reports is kept according to Google's policies (Firebase Analytics data is kept for a limited period). You can reset your advertising ID at any time.
  • Because we do not collect personal information on our own servers, there is no account or server data for us to delete. If you want the statistics linked to your app instance deleted, contact us.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the game changes. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at mohamed.abaza3296@gmail.com.
