Fragile Freight: Delivery Under Pressure

Privacy Policy. Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains what information the Fragile Freight game for Android uses, and why. The game is made by Abaza APPs ("we").

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location. We do not run our own servers, so we do not receive your game data.

Your game progress

The game saves your gems, stars, vehicles, upgrades, purchase records, number of finished runs and ad-free status in a file on your device. It also saves your best delivery time on each stage, your progress towards achievements (such as the number of deliveries, clean landings and your longest jump), and your settings, such as the menu theme and whether the ghost car is shown. This file is not sent to us.

For the ghost car, the game also keeps a recording of your fastest run on each stage: where your vehicle was and how it was tilted during the run. These recordings stay in separate files on your device. They are never sent anywhere, not even to your cloud save.

Google Play Games

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game keeps a copy of your progress in Google Play Games Saved Games, so it can follow you to a new device. Signing in is your choice, and the game works without it.

When you sign in, Google shares your Play Games profile with the game, such as your gamertag and avatar. The game uses this to find your saved progress and to show your achievements and leaderboard times. Google also collects analytics and diagnostic data for this service. Only the signed-in player's copy of the game can read this saved progress, and we cannot see it.

While you are signed in, the game also sends your unlocked achievements, your progress towards them, and your best delivery time on each stage to Google Play Games. Your times appear on that stage's leaderboard next to your Play Games gamertag and avatar. Who can see your achievements and times is set by your Play Games profile visibility (Everyone, Friends only or Only you), which you can change in your Play Games profile at any time.

You can delete your Play Games data, including your achievements and leaderboard times, in your Play Games profile or your Google account.

Android Auto Backup

If backup is turned on for your device and you are signed in to a Google account, Android may copy your game save to your private Google backup storage. This lets your progress return if you reinstall the game. Google runs this service, and we cannot see your backup. You can turn backups off in your device settings.

Advertising

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. Forced ads appear on the Results screen, and you can choose to watch a rewarded ad to double a run's pay. Small banner ads appear at the bottom of the stage list and on the pause screen. No ads appear while you drive. Any purchase turns off forced ads and banners.

Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect information from your device to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. This can include:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • device and app information, such as model and operating system
  • your IP address, which can suggest your general location
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data

Where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your choice about personalized ads before ads load. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services, and read the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. Google processes your payment, and we never see your card or bank details. The game only receives confirmation of what you bought, so it can deliver your purchase and turn off forced ads.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your game progress and ghost recordings stay on your device until you uninstall the game. To remove a backup copy, manage or delete your device backups in your Google account. To remove the Play Games copy, your achievements and your leaderboard times, delete your data in your Play Games profile or Google account. Because we do not hold your information, we cannot delete it for you. Google's handling of its data is described in its privacy policy.

Security

Information sent by Google's services is protected in transit by those services. No method of storing or sending data is completely secure.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, such as asking to see or delete it. We hold none of your information ourselves, so requests about advertising or purchase data are best made to Google. You can still contact us and we will help where we can.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. We will post the new version on this page and change the date at the top.
